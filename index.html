Select Homeworld

Your homeworld shapes your character's origins and provides characteristic modifiers. Click on an option to see details and select it.

Select Background

Your background represents your character's past and provides starting skills, talents, and characteristic bonuses.

Select Role

Your role defines your character's specialization and provides aptitudes, starting skills, and talents.

Generate Characteristics

Choose how to generate your characteristics. You can roll randomly or allocate points manually.
